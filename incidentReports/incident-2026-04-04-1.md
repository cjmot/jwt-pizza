# Incident: 2026-04-04 14-52-07

## Summary

> [!NOTE]
> Write a summary of the incident in a few sentences. Include what happened, why, the severity of the incident and how
> long the impact lasted.

Between 2:45pm and 2:54pm on April 4th 2026, a major level issue was identified and resolved.
The issue was identified as pizza creation being denied by the pizza factory. All users attempting to buy a pizza in
that time frame were unable to do so.

No other user interactions were affected other than pizza creation.

## Detection

> [!NOTE]
> When did the team detect the incident? How did they know it was happening? How could we improve time-to-detection?
> Consider: How would we have cut that time by half?

This incident was detected when the pizza creation alert group was triggered by an abnormal number of pizza creation
failures. Cooper Motyer was paged at 2:52pm as soon as the alert group was triggered and was online and resolved the
issue by 2:54pm.

Pending time before an alert group is registered will be cut from 5 minutes to 2 minutes to improve detection time in
the future.

For 9 minutes between 14:45pm and 14:54pm MST on 04/04/2026, our users were unable to buy pizzas.

No support cases were raised.

## Response

> [!NOTE]
> Who responded to the incident? When did they respond, and what did they do? Note any delays or obstacles to
> responding.

After receiving a page at 14:52 MST, Cooper Motyer checked pizza-service logs in grafana to verify issue and begin
resolving. He saw that the response from the pizza factory included "chaos monkey" and a link to resolve the issue. He
followed the link (after verifying it was a clean link) and resolved the issue by 14:54 MST.

## Root cause

> [!NOTE]
> Note the final root cause of the incident, the thing identified that needs to change in order to prevent this class of
> incident from happening again.

The pizza factory randomly triggered a chaos monkey event that stopped all pizza creation and returned requests to the
pizza factory with a 500 error and a url to resolve the issue. This was a test of response time for our team and to
ensure effective communication with the pizza factory.

## Resolution

> [!NOTE]
> Describe how the service was restored and the incident was deemed over. Detail how the service was successfully
> restored and you knew how what steps you needed to take to recovery.
> Depending on the scenario, consider these questions: How could you improve time to mitigation? How could you have cut
> that time by half?

Following the link provided in the pizza factory response triggered the end of the chaos test. Users were immediately
able to buy pizzas again.

Time to mitigation could be improved by having the alert group trigger earlier. As it stood, no one was notified about
the failures until 10 minutes after the issue began.
